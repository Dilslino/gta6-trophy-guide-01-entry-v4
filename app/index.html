Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There are no online trophies, no difficulty setting, and no mission gold medals. Keep cheats off for the whole run: a cheat disables trophies and saving. Rotate manual saves at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave) because there is no mission replay.

  1. 1 Finish the story · 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters in order and do the side questlines as they are marked: Kenton’s treasure dives, Billy’s Mud Club jobs, Andy’s Cellerica line for Lucia, Randy’s sports-car wishlist, Tyrone’s lab raids for Jason, Bret’s hunts. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study every new animal through binoculars as you go; both counters feed the 100% checklist later.

    Do this in Chapter 1 or never: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony, and choose Accept when she offers to teach you to meditate. It is the only missable-adjacent moment in the game.

  2. 2 The 100% checklist · ~100 hours

    After the credits, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: any 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 of 397 stunts, 15 of 18 meditations, any 70 of 80 challenges. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races, and Bret’s quests demand everything. Nothing on the list is missable; all of it is doable in free-roam.

  3. 3 Strangers & cleanup · ~10 hours

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and more), then sweep the one-off trophies: the Ocean View Martini, the blue Banshee showroom smash, the Starlet Motel UFO, the VCIA plane theft, and the Mile Zero tank. Two trophies are still unconfirmed at launch (Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin); their entries say so plainly and give the best lead instead of guessing.

The missable wall

Verified against PowerPyx and GTABase at launch: there is exactly one trophy that can be threatened by story progress. Everything else can be earned in free-roam after the credits. Protect this one and the platinum can never be locked out.

Sources & honesty notes

Every guide in this atlas is written in our own words for this entry, and every trophy names its sources beside the guide: PowerPyx (GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap) and GTABase (GTA 6 Trophy List) first, with the trophy names, descriptions, and grades cross-checked against PSNProfiles and KeenGamer listings of the same 55. A cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed — so where the sources disagree or stay silent, this atlas says so instead of guessing. Two trophies, Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin, have no confirmed unlock method at launch; their entries state that plainly and give the best lead. Three more were confirmed on launch day (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, The Truth Is Out There) and are marked RESOLVED. This is an unofficial fan guide. It contains no Rockstar Games logo, artwork, or game screenshots, and it fetches nothing from the network: it runs entirely from its own files.
